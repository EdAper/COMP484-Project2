New here? A tiny guide to a happy pet.

Give Dogey treats, play a game, or head out for exercise. Play needs 12 energy and exercise needs 18. Let Dogey nap for 6 seconds to recover 30 energy and 5 happiness. Clicking Wake up ends a nap early without the recovery bonus.

Your first click on Dogey gives a one-time +10 happiness bonus. Happiness and energy stay between 0 and 100. Weight stays at or above 0. Start over resets the game and makes the greeting bonus available again.

Progress saves in this browser when storage is available. A page reload wakes Dogey; unfinished naps do not earn a bonus. You can also use Tab and Enter or Space to play.
